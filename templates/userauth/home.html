How it works

From registration to results, every step is designed to protect your identity and your vote.

Create your account

Sign up and verify your email. Optionally complete eligibility (e.g. age, citizenship) so you can be invited to the elections you’re allowed to vote in.

Cast your ballot

Open an active election, choose your options (single choice, multiple choice, or ranked), and submit. Your choices are encrypted before they are stored.

Verify and trust the result

After voting you receive a receipt. Use it later to confirm your vote was counted. Results are published only when the organiser chooses, with full auditability.

Why VAULT

Security and transparency are built in, not bolted on.

Encrypted votes

Ballot choices are encrypted so only authorised tallying can use them.

Verifiable receipts

Check that your vote was included without revealing how you voted.

Audit trails

Election and security logs support accountability and compliance.

Invite-based access

Voters can be invited by email so only eligible people see the ballot.

Your vote stays yours

We don’t link your identity to your choices in a way that allows anyone to see how you voted. Elections can require email verification, optional MFA, and CAPTCHA to reduce abuse. Organisers control who can vote and when results are published.
